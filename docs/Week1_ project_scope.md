**ResearchLens Project Scope**  
By: Mun Weng Yann A24AI0067

| Project Name | ResearchLens \- AI Research Paper Discovery & Summarisation Assistant |
| :---- | :---- |
| Domain | Academic Research and Scholarly Information Retrieval  |
| Problem Statement | University students and early-stage researchers need to locate relevant academic papers when conducting assignments, literature reviews, and research projects. Finding suitable papers and reviewing many abstracts can require considerable effort, particularly when users are unfamiliar with academic search tools or research terminology. |
| Evidence of the Problem | - **Difficulty finding academic information:** Shuhidan et al. (2019) examined difficulties experienced by Malaysian university students in locating and evaluating information for academic purposes. [Research paper](https://ibimapublishing.com/articles/JELHE/2019/822970/) <br><br> - **Differences in academic search performance:** Gusenbauer and Haddaway (2020) evaluated 28 academic search systems and identified differences in their ability to retrieve research. [Research paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7079055/) <br><br> - **Information overload:** Arnold et al. (2023) reviewed literature on information overload and the challenges associated with managing large amounts of information. [Research paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10322198/) |
| Initial Related Work | - **OpenAlex** — An open scholarly catalogue and API for searching academic works and retrieving metadata. [Source](https://openalex.org/) <br><br> - **Semantic Scholar** — An academic search platform providing paper details, abstracts, and citation information when available. [Source](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) <br><br> - **arXiv** — A public repository of research papers with an API for searching records and retrieving metadata. [Source](https://arxiv.org/) |
| Engineering Gap/ Limitation | - **OpenAlex** — Abstracts and full-text documents are not available for every record, so some papers cannot be summarised. <br><br> - **Semantic Scholar** — Some papers have missing abstracts or unavailable full-text documents. <br><br> - **arXiv** — Its coverage focuses on specific research fields, and preprints are not necessarily peer-reviewed.  |
| Initial Project Objectives | - **O1:** To develop a Streamlit application that retrieves academic papers from OpenAlex based on research topics and selected filters. <br><br> - **O2:** To implement abstract summarisation using a locally run pretrained NLP model, with a Python-based extractive summarisation fallback. |
| Target Users | University students preparing assignments, research proposals or final-year projects; postgraduate students beginning literature reviews; and early-stage researchers exploring new research topics. |
| Data Sources | - **OpenAlex Works API:** Live academic paper metadata, available abstracts, publication years, authors and source links. [API URL](https://api.openalex.org/works) <br><br> - **arXiv:** Public research papers and metadata for selected test cases and evaluation. [API Documentation](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/index.html) <br><br> - **Semantic Scholar API:** Academic paper metadata for optional comparison and cross-checking during evaluation. [API Documentation](https://api.semanticscholar.org/api-docs/) |
| Similar to EduAdvisor | Both systems help users find and understand information through a focused application. Both also require source transparency and systematic evaluation. |
| Different from EduAdvisor | ResearchLens focuses on academic paper discovery and abstract summarisation using scholarly metadata and natural language processing (NLP), rather than answering university academic-policy questions from official documents. |
| Responsible AI Risks | - **Risk 1 — Inaccurate summaries:** The summarisation model may omit important details or produce unsupported statements. **Control:** Display the original abstract alongside the summary and evaluate summary faithfulness. <br><br> - **Risk 2 — Incomplete research coverage:** Some papers may have missing abstracts, incomplete metadata or unavailable full text. **Control:** Display available source links and identify missing information. <br><br> - **Risk 3 — Overreliance on summaries:** Users may rely on summaries without reading the original research. **Control:** Remind users to verify important claims against the original paper. <br><br> - **Risk 4 — API key exposure:** Credentials may be accidentally published in a public repository. **Control:** Store keys in environment variables and exclude `.env` from Git. |
